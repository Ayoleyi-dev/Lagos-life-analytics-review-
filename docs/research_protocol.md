# Research protocol — public Lagos Life product intelligence

**Study date:** 17 October 2026. **Status:** exploratory pilot; not a representative study.

## Research questions
1. What public firsthand feedback highlights friction or enjoyment during the early experience?
2. What recurring product hypotheses should Vatar validate with private, consent-appropriate telemetry?
3. Which metrics and events would distinguish viral awareness from durable player engagement?

## Provenance and inclusion rules
- Source of truth for product identity: official Vatar site linking to https://lagoslife.app/.
- Public community seed: one public r/Nigeria conversation, SRC-004. Only independent statements about playing, appearance, pacing or developer updates are included.
- Manual paraphrases **are not verbatim quotes**. Read source thread to audit context and timing.
- First-person reports and observers who have not played are coded separately. `confidence` means coder certainty about classification, **not** truth or generalisability.
- Keep company claims and participant accounts distinct. Numerical player/revenue claims are excluded from this seed set because they were not independently verified.
- Sampling frame is incomplete, convenience-based and biased toward online commenters. One discussion thread is not an independent sample of players.

## Annotation rubric
- `positive`: explicit satisfaction or praise; `negative`: explicit dissatisfaction; `mixed`: both; `neutral`: non-evaluative.
- `firsthand=yes`: writer directly reports game experience (not independently verified); `secondhand_impression`: reports on screenshots or updates.
- Duplicate remarks should be excluded in future; each new source must have a source registry entry first.

## Collection and ethics
- **No automated X scraping.** X's terms prohibit unapproved scraping. A compliant connector would require approved developer access and respect redistribution limits.
- Do not scrape game APIs, authentication endpoints, chats, account profiles, payments or participant identifiers.
- Store only short paraphrases, source thread URLs, date and research codes. No handles, avatars, IP addresses or private messages.
- Respect platform rules, rate limits, deletion requests and applicable law; recheck linked content before publication.
- Do not equate promotional counts with independent proof of players or retention.

## Validation plan
Expand to independent permitted sources; preregister coding guide, second-annotator review and agreement metrics. For actual retention or economy analysis, require Vatar authorisation and privacy-reviewed, aggregated telemetry.
