# Trust and safety watch — external reporting, not direct player telemetry

As of **17 October 2026**, [FIJ's 11 October reporting](https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-story-viral-lagos-life-game-updates-access-policy-for-adult-content/) describes the platform adding an 18+ age-access requirement and removing advertisements following reporting about inappropriate third-party advertisements. That report also describes the operator's statements about impersonation and fraudulent messages. These are time-specific **attributed media reports** and should not be reframed as independently observed current incidents or an assessment of legal compliance.

The game offers an [official issue-reporting route](https://lagoslife.app/report). For a potential analytics project, track only the safe operational metrics needed to improve a consent-aware reporting pipeline: aggregated submission categories, acknowledged vs resolved cases, and time-to-resolution. Do not harvest player chats, explore vulnerabilities or test other players' accounts; report safety problems through the operator's process. Preserve privacy and avoid publishing identifying details.

**Data governance:** this source is contextual desk research, **not** coded player sentiment and **not** an internal audit. No claim is made that the reporter's described problems are currently unresolved.
